**ECONnect — KU Leuven (2024)**  
*Insurance Analytics for a Changing Risk Landscape*

Interview in *ECONnect* (December 2024) on actuarial science, changing risks, and the role of data analytics in insurance.

**Junior College KU Leuven (2024)**  
*Kansrekening en financieel waarderen: de actuaris aan het werk*

A lecture for secondary school students introducing actuarial science, probability, financial mathematics, and the role of actuaries in banking and insurance.

The session explores how mathematical and statistical ideas are used to understand risk, insurance, pensions, and financial decisions.

[Watch the lecture →](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l34aGKMqWP4&t=2624s)  
[Slides →](/files/portfolio/junior_college_2024.pdf)  
[Junior College learning module →](https://www.kuleuven.be/onderwijs/juniorcollege/leermodules/stem/overzicht#geld)
